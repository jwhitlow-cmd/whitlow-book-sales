Reports

Profit is an estimate. Opening inventory has no assigned cost and can overstate profit; purchases are counted as inventory until sold. Other expenses are deducted. KDP earnings are recorded as net royalties, not gross Amazon customer sales.

Backup & device privacy

This free app stores records only in this browser on this device. It has no cloud account, automatic backup, or encrypted database. Export a JSON backup after every sales event or at least weekly. Save it to iCloud Drive or another safe location. A browser data clear or device loss may otherwise erase your records.

Restoring replaces all current records after confirmation. Download a backup of your current data first.
